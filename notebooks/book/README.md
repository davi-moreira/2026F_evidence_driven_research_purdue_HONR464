# EDR|AI — book companion notebooks

One Colab workbook per chapter of **EDR|AI — Evidence-Driven Research in the
Age of AI** ([read the book](https://davi-moreira.github.io/2026F_evidence_driven_research_purdue_HONR464/book/index.html)).
These notebooks belong to the **book**, not to the course: each carries its
chapter's AI prompts, runnable code, and the closing **"It is your turn"**
workspace, so a reader can complete the whole book from the chapter badges
with nothing but free Colab.

- `chNN_*.ipynb` — English edition
- `pt/chNN_*.ipynb` — Português (Brasil)
- `es/chNN_*.ipynb` — Español

The course's own weekly lab notebooks are a different artifact and live in
[`notebooks/student/`](../student/).

Generated from the chapter sources by `scripts/build_book_notebooks.py` —
do not edit these files by hand; edit the chapter and rebuild.
