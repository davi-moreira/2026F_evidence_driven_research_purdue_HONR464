## The proposal, screen by screen (from the filed PDF)

The course-based proposal asks for **no project content at all** — no title, no
abstract, no question, no mentor. It is an enrollment declaration and takes about
five minutes.

1. **Contact Information** — first name, last name, email.
   *Exception:* a student with a "request to restrict directory information"
   (confidential restriction on the Purdue record) cannot submit normally and must
   email **`scholarlyproject@purdue.edu`** for alternative arrangements.
2. **Educational Background** — Disciplinary College (primary), Major(s),
   Expected Graduation, **Honors Advisor**, JMHC Location. All dropdowns; students
   need to know their JMHC advisor's name before they start.
3. **Project Type** — five choices. Students in this course **must pick
   "HONR Course Approved to Fulfill the Scholarly Project Requirement (i.e.,
   HONR 46x)"**. The deck flags this in red. The trap is the last button,
   *"I am not ready to propose at this time"* — a student who is unsure about
   their project will reach for it and will not have proposed.
4. **Project-based Coursework** — acknowledge the approved-course list, then pick
   the **semester** in which the course is being taken (Fall 2026), then
   **Acknowledge and Submit**. That completes the proposal.

JMHC offers the **Research in Action Showcase, Dec 7–11, 2026**, and asks
instructors who want time reserved for their students to reply to Sarah Vaughn by
**Mon Nov 23, 2026**.

This course instead presents at the **Purdue Fall Undergraduate Research Expo on
Tue Nov 17, 2026** — required and graded (`syllabus.qmd:152`, `:167`;

or whether JMHC expects course-based projects at the December Showcase, is not

and Sarah. **Do not let Nov 23 pass without an answer.**
