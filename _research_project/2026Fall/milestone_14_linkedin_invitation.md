## About the Research Project

Your semester project is **individual by default**: one researcher, one question,
carried from curiosity to a defended, reproducible claim. With instructor
approval, and only if at least three active projects remain and the required
peer-evaluation assignments are feasible, you may instead complete the same
chain as a group. The same five Final Project components apply
in either mode. Unless this brief marks a requirement as individual, an approved
group submits one shared artifact naming every member, and its shared rubric
rows receive a common score. Each member's AI Research Ledger and any other
requirement marked individual are scored per member, so recorded milestone
scores can differ only on those rows. Every member remains responsible for the
complete work and their own defense. Peer Evaluation remains a true peer
rating: group members rate every teammate; an individual-project researcher
is rated by two instructor-assigned project peers; nobody rates themselves. See
[Final Project — Grading and Project Modes](final_project_grading_and_project_modes.md).
It runs through milestones
**M1–M17**, peaks publicly at the **Purdue Fall Undergraduate Research Expo
poster session (Tuesday, November 17 — required)**, and closes with the
reproducible package and cold run in December. Milestone weights live in
[Final Project — Grading and Project Modes](final_project_grading_and_project_modes.md); instructions and
rubrics live one page per milestone, like this one.

Research is finished when someone reads it, and an audience does not assemble on
its own. In this milestone you **publish a professional LinkedIn post inviting
your network** to your poster presentation at the Expo: classmates, friends,
family, mentors, and anyone who might care about your question.

## What the Post Must Carry

Copy these details **exactly as the official Expo program lists them**. Do not
write them from memory: an invitation with the wrong room is a missed audience.

- **Your poster title**
- **Your name**, and the names of every co-author if yours is an approved group
- **The date and time** of your presentation
- **The location**: building and room or space

In addition, the post must:

- be written in a **professional tone** suitable for a public audience;
- make clear that you are presenting at the **Purdue Fall Undergraduate Research
  Expo**, as part of your research in HONR 46400 at the John Martinson Honors
  College;
- **invite your network to attend** and visit your poster.

You may add a visual (a photo of you, a detail of your poster's main figure, or a
project graphic), a sentence or two on what your project found, and hashtags. You
may tag me: <https://www.linkedin.com/in/davimoreira/>. If you describe your
result, describe it at its true size: the post is one more place where a claim
can quietly grow.

### Example (yours does not need to follow it exactly)

> I am excited to share that I will be presenting my research at the **Purdue
> Fall Undergraduate Research Expo**! My project, developed in HONR 46400
> Evidence-Driven Research at the John Martinson Honors College and mentored by
> Prof. Davi Moreira, asks [your question, in one sentence].
>
> **Poster title:** [exactly as in the program]
> **Date and time:** [exactly as in the program]
> **Location:** [building and room or space, exactly as in the program]
>
> I would be delighted to see you there and to talk about what the evidence
> shows, and where it stops. #Purdue #HonorsCollege #UndergraduateResearch
> #Boilerup

If you cannot post on LinkedIn, write to me **before the deadline** and we agree
on an alternative public invitation.

## How It Is Graded

M14 is graded on **completion**, and its score is one of the seventeen averaged
inside **Milestone Deliverables**:

| Outcome | Score |
|---|---:|
| The post is published and the screenshot shows every required element, copied exactly from the program | 100 |
| The post is published, but a required element is missing or does not match the program | 50 |
| No post was published, or no screenshot was submitted | 0 |

**Late:** −10 points per day, up to 3 days; not accepted after (documented
emergencies handled individually per Purdue policy). There is no revision window:
an invitation is useful only before the Expo.

If you use AI to draft or polish the post, add a row to your **AI Research
Ledger** naming what you changed and how you checked the program details.

---

## Three Traps

1. **The remembered room number.** You write the location from memory, the
   program says a different hall, and the people you invited spend your session
   looking for you. Copy the program entry.
2. **The post that oversells.** "My research proves…" in a post about a finding
   with an interval. Describe the result the way your poster does, or describe
   the question instead.
3. **The draft you never published.** A screenshot of a post still in the editor
   is not a published post. Publish it, then take the screenshot.
