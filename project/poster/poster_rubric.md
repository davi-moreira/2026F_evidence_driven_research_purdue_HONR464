# Poster Quality Rubric — moved

The single, authoritative poster rubric is the **Final Project poster-quality rubric** in
[course milestone M13 — Final Poster Lock](../../_research_project/2026Fall/milestone_13_final_poster_lock.md).

Grade the locked poster against that rubric's five criteria (Compass & pathway
alignment, Evidence integrity & provenance, Verification & traceability,
Uncertainty & limitations, and Craft, ledger & communication — the last of which
carries the AI Research Ledger requirement and its accessibility criterion). Do
not maintain a second poster rubric here; this file exists only to point you
there.

The locked poster and its public delivery supply the Final Project's **Poster
and Expo Presentation** item (20% of Final Project, or 6 course points): `70%`
comes from the M13 poster-quality rubric and `30%` from the M15 live Expo
presentation rubric. The
poster also enters the holistic **Instructor Evaluation and Evidence Defense**
item. M13's separate process table grades the on-time, gate-cleared lock; no
poster-quality score is copied into the milestone category.
