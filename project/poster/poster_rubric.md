# Poster Rubric — moved

The single, authoritative poster rubric is the **Final Project poster rubric** in
[course milestone M11 — Final Poster](../../_research_project/2026Fall/milestone_11_final_poster.md).
Since D90 (2026-10-26) it grades the **final poster submitted at M11**; M13 only
locks the print file, with the repairs the M12 peer review found.

Grade the M11 final poster against that rubric's eight criteria, one per group of
template sections: question, context & contribution; target, design & warranted
claims; research design & methodology; checks & verification; results & figure
honesty; interpretation, uncertainty & limitations; sources, AI disclosure &
credit; and visual communication & accessibility. For an approved group the
poster score is common. Each member's personally attributable AI Research Ledger
is graded instead in the individual row of the M11 process score. Do not maintain
a second poster rubric here; this file exists only to point you there.

The same rubric also ships as documents students hold and graders fill in:
[`Evidence_Driven_Research_Poster_Rubric.pdf`](Evidence_Driven_Research_Poster_Rubric.pdf)
(with `.docx` and the `.xlsx` grading sheet), next to the course template
[`honr464_poster_template.pptx`](honr464_poster_template.pptx). All of them, and
M11's rubric block, are generated from one source by
`scripts/build_poster_kit.py`; edit the script, never the files.

The M11 final poster and its public delivery supply the Final Project's **Poster
Presentation at the Purdue Undergraduate Research Conference** item (16% of
the 75-point Final Project, or 12 points of the course grade, D79): `70%` comes
from the poster rubric on the M11 final poster (8.4 course points) and `30%` from
the M15 live Expo presentation rubric (3.6 course points). For an approved group
project, the poster score is common, but every member must present and receives
their own live-presentation score. The poster locked at M13 enters the evidence
base for **Instructor/TA Evaluation**. M11's and M13's process tables supply their
scores inside **Milestone Deliverables**; no poster-rubric score is copied into
either process score.
