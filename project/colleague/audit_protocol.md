# The Colleague Audit — in-class protocol (18–22 minutes)

You run this protocol on your colleague's current artifact inside the
normal Monday investigation block or Wednesday laboratory block. It is the
same seven-move sequence every time, so the moves become reflex. Bring the
artifact, your project notebook, and the book's current Studio page.

| # | Move | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Commit** | 2′ | Before checking anything, mark each major claim or decision in the artifact `supported`, `unsupported`, or `uncertain`. Committing first is what makes calibration measurable. |
| 2 | **Locate** | 3′ | For everything you marked `unsupported` or `uncertain`, point to the exact sentence, cell, figure, or ledger row. "Something feels off in the analysis" is not a locator. |
| 3 | **Verify** | 6–8′ | Check it: retrieve the source and read it, rerun the code, inspect the data, or apply the book's rule from this Studio. One verified finding beats five suspicions. |
| 4 | **Diagnose** | 3′ | Name what the flaw IS — the misconception, the crossing violation, or the rubric criterion it fails. A flaw you can name is a flaw you will recognize in your own work. |
| 5 | **Repair** | 3′ | Write the smallest defensible correction, and the new claim boundary it implies. Do not rebuild the project; fix the decision. |
| 6 | **Calibrate** | 2′ | Defend one element that LOOKED suspicious but is actually sound, and say how you confirmed it. This move is scored — false alarms cost you. |
| 7 | **Accuracy lock** | 2′ | The SRL synthesizes the room's findings; the instructor confirms the seeded flaw and states the corrected version. Correct your record now, before anything enters a ledger. |

**After class (2 minutes, not homework):** carry one line into your own
project — the same check, applied to your own current artifact — and log
it in your AI Research Ledger.

Two standing rules:

- If you used AI during the audit (to summarize the artifact, to check a
  calculation), that use goes in your ledger like any other.
- The audit ends when the record is corrected, not when the flaw is found.
  An error left uncorrected in your notes is the one you will repeat in
  November.
