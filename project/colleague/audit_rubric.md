# Colleague Audit Rubric — four rows, 0–2 each (8 points per audit)

Scored live or from your submitted audit sheet. Your **best 10 of the 12
scored Studio audits** count inside the participation grade. The rubric
rewards verified findings and calibrated judgment — not suspicion volume.

| Row | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Locator and verdict** | No exact locator, or a verdict with no committed basis | Locator or verdict present but incomplete (a section named, not a sentence; a verdict changed after checking without saying so) | Exact locator (sentence / cell / figure / ledger row) and a committed verdict made BEFORE verification |
| **Rule and evidence** | "It seems wrong" — no rule named, nothing checked | The right rule named but the check is weak (source skimmed, code read but not run) | The named rule (misconception, crossing, or rubric criterion) PLUS a real check — source retrieved, code rerun, data inspected |
| **Repair and boundary** | No repair, or a repair with the same flaw in new clothes | Partial repair; the corrected claim boundary is missing or vague | The smallest defensible correction, with the new claim boundary stated in one sentence |
| **Calibration** | Everything flagged as wrong, or nothing defended | A sound element confirmed, but without saying how | At least one suspicious-looking element defended WITH its verification — and no false positives among your final verdicts |

**Scoring policy.**

- 12 audits are scored across the semester (one per Studio); the best 10
  count. The two unscored continuation beats (defense week, revision week)
  are practice only.
- Finding the seeded flaw is not required for a strong score: a verified,
  well-diagnosed audit that catches a DIFFERENT genuine weakness scores on
  its merits. The instructor key lists likely false positives, and the
  accuracy lock settles disputes in the room, same session.
- A missing AI Research Ledger row for AI used during the audit caps the
  audit at half marks — the same disclosure discipline as everywhere else
  in this course.
