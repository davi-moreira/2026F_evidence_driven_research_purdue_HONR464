Milestone column. Every milestone links to the Book Milestone it presents in EDR|AI. A ✚ marks a milestone where this course asks for more than that book milestone does. Its brief on Brightspace explains exactly what, and everything is submitted as one file, lastname_mNN.pdf.

Reading column. Each chapter’s “It is your turn” section is a separate submission from the milestone, graded on whether you handed it in, with its own file: LASTNAME_iyt_ch<numbers>.ipynb.
