# Student Profile Survey — the slot

**Due: Sunday Aug 30, 2026, 11:59 PM** (the end of Week 1, the same night as
Milestone 1 and the Studio 1 feedback response).

**Graded inside Participation (9%)**, for completion only — submitted or not.
One credit, exactly like every other participation item; the rule is in
[`participation_grading.md`](participation_grading.md).

**Why it opens the semester.** The course runs on individual projects, random
Student Research Lead slots, and pathway assignment. Every one of those
decisions is made better with a profile in hand, and all three land in the first
three weeks. Asking in Week 1 is not administrative throat-clearing; it is the
input to the assignments the instructor has to make by Week 5.
