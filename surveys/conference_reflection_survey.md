# Conference Reflection Survey — the instrument (M16, part 1)

**Survey name (Qualtrics and Brightspace): `Conference Reflection Survey`.**

**Status.** Hand-authored for D91 (2026-10-26), not generated.
`qualtrics_conference_reflection.txt` in this folder is the Qualtrics Advanced
Format import file and is the source of truth for the question wording; this page
describes it. Mark every question as required (Force Response) by hand in the
Qualtrics UI after import.

**Import:** Projects → Create project → Survey → **From a file** → upload
`qualtrics_conference_reflection.txt`. Post the link on Brightspace with the M16
assignment before the first class session after the Expo.

**When it is answered.** During the first of the two class sessions after the
Expo (M16 part 1). The written plan (M16 part 2, `lastname_m16.pdf`) is written in
the second session and builds on these answers. The M16 brief carries no dates;
the dates live in `course_config.yaml` and on Brightspace.

**Grading.** The survey is the *Survey completed* row (20 points) of the M16
rubric in `_research_project/2026Fall/milestone_16_conference_reflection.md`.
Answers are identified by username because they are graded.

## The questions

| ID | Type | Question |
|---|---|---|
| `student` | single line | Purdue username |
| `conference_learned` | essay | What you learned at the Expo beyond your own poster; one thing another presenter did that you would borrow, and why |
| `visitors_count` | single choice | Roughly how many people stopped (0–5 / 6–10 / 11–20 / more than 20) |
| `visitor_types` | multiple choice | Who stopped (students, faculty from far fields, people who know the topic, judges, family and friends, someone else) |
| `audience_describe` | essay | Two or three sentences on who the audience was and what they wanted from the poster |
| `questions_asked` | essay | Up to five questions remembered most clearly, from the Expo notes |
| `recurring` | essay | The question or kind of question that came up more than once, or "none recurred" |
| `target` | single choice | Whether the questions mostly aimed at communication, at claims, both, or too few to tell |
| `hardest` | essay | The hardest question, how it was answered, and what a good answer would need |
| `did_well` | essay | One thing the presentation did well, judged against the questions |
| `differently` | essay | One thing to do differently |
| `notes` | single choice | Whether the questions were written down at the Expo |

The `target` item carries the distinction the M16 plan depends on: a question
aimed at **communication** is fixed in how the work is presented, while a question
aimed at the **claims** may change the research itself.
